# Imaging and Procedures Prior Authorization Policy

> Synthetic document for Acme Health Plan, a fictional payer. Not real coverage information.

## Purpose

This policy lists the imaging tests, surgeries and related services that need prior authorization, and the criteria a request must meet. A request that meets every criterion is recommended for approval. A request that misses a criterion goes to a clinical reviewer.

## Services done without prior authorization

If a service on this list is done without an approved prior authorization, the claim is denied with code CO-197. The provider may request a retro authorization within 30 days of the denial, or the member may file an appeal.

## MRI of the lower spine (72148)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. Low back pain for at least 6 weeks
2. At least 6 weeks of conservative treatment (physical therapy or medication) tried
3. Neurological symptoms such as leg weakness or numbness, or a red-flag finding
4. No MRI of the lower spine in the past 12 months

## MRI of the knee (73721)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. Knee pain for at least 6 weeks
2. At least 6 weeks of conservative treatment (physical therapy or medication) tried
3. Knee X-ray done within the past 60 days
4. Ordered by an in-network provider
5. No MRI of the same knee in the past 12 months

## CT scan of the head (70450)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver.

Criteria:

1. New or worsening headache with a neurological finding, or recent head injury
2. Ordered after an in-person exam
3. No head CT in the past 30 days

## CT scan of the abdomen and pelvis (74177)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver.

Criteria:

1. Abdominal symptoms not explained by lab tests or ultrasound
2. Ordered after an in-person exam
3. No abdominal CT in the past 90 days

## PET scan, skull base to mid-thigh (78815)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. Confirmed or strongly suspected cancer diagnosis
2. Result will change the treatment plan
3. Ordered by an oncologist

## Knee arthroscopy with cartilage trim (29881)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. MRI shows a cartilage tear
2. Mechanical symptoms such as locking or catching
3. At least 6 weeks of physical therapy tried

## Total knee replacement (27447)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. X-ray shows severe arthritis of the knee
2. Pain limits daily activities
3. At least 3 months of conservative treatment tried
4. No active infection

## Lower back disc surgery (63030)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. MRI shows a disc problem that matches the symptoms
2. At least 6 weeks of conservative treatment tried
3. Ongoing leg pain or weakness

## Upper endoscopy with biopsy (43239)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver.

Criteria:

1. Symptoms for at least 4 weeks despite medication
2. Ordered by a gastroenterologist
3. Patient age or warning signs support the procedure

## Overnight sleep study in a lab (95810)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver.

Criteria:

1. Home sleep test was not possible or was inconclusive
2. Symptoms such as loud snoring or daytime sleepiness
3. Ordered after an in-person exam

## CPAP breathing machine (E0601)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver.

Criteria:

1. Sleep study confirms sleep apnea
2. Member agrees to a 90-day use check
3. Ordered by the treating provider
