# Bronze Plan Coverage Summary

> Synthetic document for Acme Health Plan, a fictional payer. Not real coverage information.

## Overview

The Bronze plan is an Acme Health Plan product for individuals and families. This summary explains what the plan pays and what the member pays. The plan year runs from January 1 to December 31.

## Costs

- Deductible: $7,000 per person per year.
- Coinsurance after the deductible: the member pays 40% and the plan pays 60%.
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $9,200 per person per year.
- Specialist visit copay: $90.

## Physical therapy

Physical therapy is covered up to 20 visits per calendar year, combined across all therapists. An evaluation visit counts toward the limit. Visits beyond 20 need a medical necessity review; see the Physical Therapy Prior Authorization Policy.

## Chiropractic care

Chiropractic care is not covered on the Bronze plan.

## Imaging

X-rays are covered without prior authorization. Advanced imaging such as MRI, CT and PET scans is covered when medically necessary and may require prior authorization; see the Imaging and Procedures Prior Authorization Policy.

## Specialty drugs

Specialty drugs given by injection or infusion are covered when prior authorization is approved; see the Specialty Drug Prior Authorization Policy.
