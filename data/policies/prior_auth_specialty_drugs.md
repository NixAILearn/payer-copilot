# Specialty Drug Prior Authorization Policy

> Synthetic document for Acme Health Plan, a fictional payer. Not real coverage information.

## Purpose

Specialty drugs need prior authorization on all plans. Approvals last 12 months and must be renewed with an update from the prescriber.

## Adalimumab injection (specialty drug) (J0135)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. Diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis or another approved condition
2. Tried at least one standard medication first
3. Negative tuberculosis screening in the past 12 months
4. Prescribed by a rheumatologist or other specialist

## Infliximab infusion (specialty drug) (J1745)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. Diagnosis of an approved inflammatory condition
2. Tried at least one standard medication first
3. Negative tuberculosis screening in the past 12 months

## Bevacizumab infusion (specialty drug) (J9035)

Prior authorization is required on: Bronze, Silver, Gold.

Criteria:

1. Confirmed cancer diagnosis for an approved use
2. Prescribed by an oncologist
3. Treatment plan attached
4. Recent lab results attached
