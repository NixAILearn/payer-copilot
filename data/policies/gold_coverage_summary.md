# Gold Plan Coverage Summary

> Synthetic document for Acme Health Plan, a fictional payer. Not real coverage information.

## Overview

The Gold plan is an Acme Health Plan product for individuals and families. This summary explains what the plan pays and what the member pays. The plan year runs from January 1 to December 31.

## Costs

- Deductible: $1,500 per person per year.
- Coinsurance after the deductible: the member pays 20% and the plan pays 80%.
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $6,000 per person per year.
- Specialist visit copay: $40.

## Physical therapy

Physical therapy is covered up to 40 visits per calendar year, combined across all therapists. An evaluation visit counts toward the limit. Visits beyond 40 need a medical necessity review; see the Physical Therapy Prior Authorization Policy.

## Chiropractic care

Chiropractic care is covered up to 20 visits per calendar year.

## Imaging

X-rays are covered without prior authorization. Advanced imaging such as MRI, CT and PET scans is covered when medically necessary and may require prior authorization; see the Imaging and Procedures Prior Authorization Policy.

## Specialty drugs

Specialty drugs given by injection or infusion are covered when prior authorization is approved; see the Specialty Drug Prior Authorization Policy.

## Services that need prior authorization

- Total knee replacement (27447)
- Knee arthroscopy with cartilage trim (29881)
- Lower back disc surgery (63030)
- MRI of the lower spine (72148)
- MRI of the knee (73721)
- PET scan, skull base to mid-thigh (78815)
- Adalimumab injection (specialty drug) (J0135)
- Infliximab infusion (specialty drug) (J1745)
- Bevacizumab infusion (specialty drug) (J9035)

## Not covered

- Cosmetic procedures
- Weight loss programs
- Services from out-of-network providers, except emergencies
