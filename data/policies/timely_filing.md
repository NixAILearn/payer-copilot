# Timely Filing Rule

> Synthetic document for Acme Health Plan, a fictional payer. Not real coverage information.

## Filing limit

In-network providers must submit claims within 90 days of the service date. Claims received later are denied with code CO-29.

## Exceptions

A late claim can be paid if the provider shows proof of an earlier on-time submission, such as a clearinghouse report, or if another insurer was billed first in good faith.

## Member impact

Members cannot be billed for claims denied for timely filing by in-network providers.
