# Physical Therapy Prior Authorization Policy

> Synthetic document for Acme Health Plan, a fictional payer. Not real coverage information.

## Visit limits

Physical therapy does not need prior authorization while the member is within the plan's yearly visit limit: Bronze 20 visits, Silver 30 visits, Gold 40 visits.

## Visits beyond the limit

Extra visits need a medical necessity review. The therapist sends a progress note showing measurable improvement and a plan for the extra visits.

## Physical therapy exercise session (97110)

Prior authorization is required on: no plan.

Criteria:

1. Visits are within the plan's yearly limit
2. Visits beyond the limit need a progress note showing improvement
3. Ordered by a treating provider
